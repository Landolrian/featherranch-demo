Gundog Training

The goal is to teach your dog the rules of the game of hunting, taking a dog from training to an actual hunt. Dogs learn obedience, marking, flushing or pointing, and retrieving, then are collar-conditioned and cross-trained on upland and waterfowl.

Owner Training Program

For owners who want to train their own dog to its highest level. One-on-one lessons teach training as a game, built on behavioral principles that are easy to understand, and never in a way that violates your conscience. Graduates have gone on to train companion dogs, gun dogs, hunt test dogs and national competitors.
