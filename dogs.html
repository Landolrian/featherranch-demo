Started & Trained Dogs

For those who don't want to start with a puppy, we have Labradors at various levels of training, from young dogs with some leash work and a few birds shot over them to experienced hunters capable of blind retrieves. Most are between 6 months and 3 years old.

Lease a trained Labrador

For hunters who don't have a dog of their own, or can't hunt with theirs on certain occasions, Feather Ranch leases trained Labrador Retrievers for a set period of time.

Short season

Month Lease

For the hunter with a short season who'd like to enjoy more of the companionship along with the hunting.

All season

Season Lease

Hunt a variety of birds through the entire season and enjoy the companionship of a people-pleasing Labrador.

A second chance

Adopt a Dog

From time to time we have dogs available for new homes: retired members of our breeding program, dogs that didn't make it as gun dogs, and older dogs who need a new home. They're generally offered for puppy price or less.
