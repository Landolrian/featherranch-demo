Our Dogs

Started, stud,lease & adopt.

Started and trained Labs, proven sires, dogs to lease for the season, and grown dogs ready for a new home.

Skip the puppy stage

Started & trained dogs.

For those who don't want to start with a puppy, we have Labradors at various levels of training, from young dogs with some leash work and a few birds shot over them, all the way to experienced hunters capable of blind retrieves. Most are between 6 months and 3 years old.

Proven sires

Stud dogs.

Titled, health-tested Labradors. Call for current stud dogs and details.

No dog this season?

Lease a trained Lab.

For hunters without a dog of their own, or who can't hunt with theirs on certain occasions, we lease trained Labrador Retrievers for a set period of time.

01

Day

For the hunter who wants to shoot birds only occasionally, with a retriever trained to work upland or waterfowl.

02

Month

For the hunter with a short season who'd like to enjoy more of the companionship along with the hunting.

03

Season

Hunt a variety of birds through the entire season and enjoy the companionship of a people-pleasing Labrador.

A second chance

Adopt a dog.

From time to time we have dogs available for new homes: retired members of our breeding program, dogs that didn't make it as gun dogs, and older dogs who need a new home. They're generally offered for puppy price or less.
