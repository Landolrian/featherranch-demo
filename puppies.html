Questions

Puppy FAQ

How much is the deposit?

A $100 deposit holds a puppy for you. Call (209) 826-1568 for current puppy prices.

How is pick of the litter decided?

Pick order is set by the order in which we receive deposits. The earlier your deposit, the earlier your pick.

What health guarantee do you offer?

Each puppy is sold with a written guarantee for hip dysplasia and hereditary eye disorders. Parents' OFA and eye results are listed with each litter.

Are your puppies good family dogs, or just hunting dogs?

Both. Our Labs are bred to be athletic gun dogs and loveable family companions, and many of our parents are both.

Can you train my puppy after I take it home?

Yes. Our Gundog, Obedience and Owner Training programs are a natural next step. Gundog training usually begins between 7 and 10 months of age.

Want the next pick?

Deposits set the pick order, so the sooner you call, the better your pick.
