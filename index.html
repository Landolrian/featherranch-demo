Welcome to Feather Ranch

Sound, smart, beautiful Labs with a love of the retrieve.

We raise physically sound puppies with good looks, intelligence and great temperaments. Whether you want an athletic gun dog or a lovable family companion, we have the pup to meet your needs.

  • Black, yellow, chocolate and fox-red puppies
  • Dew claws removed, shots up to date and wormed before going home
  • Socialized and introduced to birds
  • Started and fully trained dogs for those who'd rather skip puppyhood
